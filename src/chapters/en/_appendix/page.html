Appendix · Author’s Note

Author’s Note

Bitcoin has existed for more than a decade. But the same questions keep recurring: what exactly is Bitcoin, and how does its system work?

Bitscriptum was born from there. From the frustration of seeing so many assumptions circulating, misunderstandings passed down from one person to another, and information that creates more confusion than clarity.

I didn’t build this to convince anyone of anything.

What’s here is how this system works, technically, honestly, and as far as possible without an agenda. Because “trust” is a word that has been said far too much. We hear it everywhere, about everything, until at some point the word loses its own weight. We’re experiencing a crisis of trust not because we trust too little, but precisely because we’re asked to trust too often.

I’d rather you read, then verify for yourself. Run the code. Check the references. Don’t stop at a single source, including this platform.

Bitcoin is designed to not require trust. It’s only fitting that the way you learn it should be too.

Making Bitscriptum was one of the things I enjoyed most. I hope it’s useful for you who are searching for answers — not a belief borrowed from someone else, but an understanding you build yourself.

Maxima Freiman. 2026

Appendix · About

About

A short note about me.

I studied Bitcoin through the Bitcoin Protocol Development curriculum from Chaincode Labs, and became a contributor and member of Bitcoin Indonesia.

Bitscriptum is the result of that learning process. Made because of questions that didn’t get honest enough answers elsewhere.

A note on data: the snapshot figures in this book — price, hashrate, difficulty, block height, node software distribution, and dataset sizes — were captured at various points across 2024-2025 and are not automatically updated. The principles and protocol rules explained here do not depend on those figures.
